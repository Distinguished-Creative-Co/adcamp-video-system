# Music licenses

Only use music you are licensed to use in your videos (and in ads, if you run them).
Music found with AVS's music search (say a vibe to Claude) is added here by itself, with its creator, source and
license. A CC BY track needs a credit: AVS puts it at the end of your caption draft, and it goes wherever you post
that video. Open the source link to check a track's license yourself.
Adding your own track: check that track's own terms, and that they cover paid ads if you run ads. Free and paid music
sites each have their own rules, and some require a credit or a subscription.

To add a track you have a license for: drag it into the chat with Claude and say where it's from. Claude puts it
here, adds a row below, and sets it on the video you're making. It plays only on the videos you choose it for.
