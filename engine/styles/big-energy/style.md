# Big Energy (a starter look)

Bold one-or-two-word captions, zoom punches and big words. Fast and loud.

Uses your brand fonts and color, so it already looks like yours. Make it yours: say what to change ("calmer captions", "less movement"), or build your own from reels you love.

## Captions
- One or two words at a time, UPPERCASE, outlined, popping in. Key words in your brand color.

## The open
- Your hook words big behind your head, then a flash into the rest.

## Pace and framing
- Zoom punches on the words that matter, a whip at every cut, every other take tighter.
- Punchy color, more contrast.

## Extras it reaches for
- Giant Words on your strongest line, Snap Zoom, Word Stamp, Keycaps for your keyword, Icon Strike when you say no to something, Giant Number on the 1, 2, 3 of a list.
