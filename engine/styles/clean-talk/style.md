# Clean Talk (a starter look)

One word at a time in your brand color, low and clean, with a hook title over your head at the start. The everyday look.

Uses your brand fonts and color, so it already looks like yours. Make it yours: say what to change ("calmer captions", "less movement"), or build your own from reels you love.

## Captions
- One word at a time, lowercase, bold, in your main font and your brand color, low in the frame. No box behind them.
- Words cut straight in, no animation, so the talking carries the pace.

## The open
- A hook title over your head while you say your first line: two or three of your own words.
- A warm flash where the hook hands over to the rest.

## Pace and framing
- Full frame, you in the middle. Every other take drawn a little tighter, so long takes still move.
- Bright and natural, a touch of contrast.

## Extras it reaches for
- A clean content card when a part of your video is a list, Word Stamp on one big word, List Tracker for numbered points, Highlighter or Page Scroll when you send a screenshot.
