# Show and Tell (a starter look)

Clear captions on a dark strip plus graphics for your numbers, lists and screenshots. For teaching and ads.

Uses your brand fonts and color, so it already looks like yours. Make it yours: say what to change ("calmer captions", "less movement"), or build your own from reels you love.

## Captions
- Short lines on a dark strip, easy to read with the sound off. Key words in your brand color.

## The open
- A hook title over your head while you say your first line.

## Pace and framing
- Steady: no zooms, a zoom move at the first cut, every other take a little tighter.

## Extras it reaches for
- Every number and list you say gets shown: Big Number, Pie, List Tracker, Receipt, a content card.
- Screenshots you send: Page Scroll, Live Markup, Highlighter. Your keyword: Pinned Line, Comment Box at the end.
- In a paid ad, a pinned "Click Learn More" takes the place of the keyword and the comment box.
