Set up this video

Pick your captions, up to four effects and a color, add your script if you have one, then press Start. Claude cuts it, and your storyboard opens next.

Step 1

Captions

Pick one. Hover over a card, or press play, to watch it.

Step 2

Effects

Up to four. Two to four works best, or none for captions only. Claude places each one where your words fit it, and skips one nothing fits.

Step 3

Color

The color of your footage. Your captions and graphics keep your brand color either way.

Step 4

Your script

Optional. Paste what you meant to say, a blank line between ideas. It tells Claude where to cut and what the captions say.

Step 5

The rest

Pick a caption style
